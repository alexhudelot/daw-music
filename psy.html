Written 2026-10-29, against Infected Mushroom, eight tracks: 30 s previews of eight tracks from their records after 2001.

Fungal Logic sits inside the eight later Infected Mushroom references on 26 of 28 measures, nearest to Cities of the Future and Becoming Insane: its loudness, its balance below 3.5 kHz, how busy it is and how long its hits ring are all theirs. What sets it apart points where listening did. Its loudest 12 s change less from bar to bar than any of the eight (2.0 dB, against 2.2 to 7.6), and more of its pitch sits on three notes than in six of them: a riff circling its root, the old-school goa its melody was heard as, not their mutating lines. It is also duller, narrower and fuller up top than most of them (6.7 dB less in the highs, mids 5.4 dB narrower, holes in 19% of the highs' frames against their 38%), and more squashed than seven of the eight.
