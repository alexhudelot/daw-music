daw music

Songs written as code, as text files of notes and settings, and rendered by daw, a headless DAW in Rust, on its own synthesisers and effects. The same file gives the same audio every time. Each one has a page that measures it against the records it was after, says what it gets right and wrong, and lists what the next one should do better.

Live stream demo: psytrance synthesised in your browser while you hear it, steered by your answers.
