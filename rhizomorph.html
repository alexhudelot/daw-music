Self-assessment

Written 2026-10-30, against Scorb, 2004–2026: Scorb's own tracks, from Scorby Snacks (2004) and the album Ipso Fvcto (2007) to Aecidium (2026): 30 s previews of 19 tracks.

Rhizomorph is the first track made with the DAW's own synth (F33), sidechain (F32), automation (F34), atmosphere (F25) and spaces (F26), written against Sporadic's review. Every sound is a patch in the song: the kick a sine falling 66 semitones and gone by 95 ms; the bass two saws and a sine through a lowpass plucked by an envelope, its first 16th at 103 ms, after the kick, and ducked by the kick's own sound; the call saws through a formant filter whose vowel an envelope closes, folded; the acid, the FM chirps and bloops, the hard-synced lead, the haze, the chords, the whistle, the wind and the air. It builds for 16 bars into the first drop and 8 into the second: the call teased under a lowpass that opens, a snare roll, a riser and the build bus pitched up, the master's highpass rising, the tape echo's feedback past 1, then the last bar frozen in the space and the hall with the chords cut and a 16th of silence before the drop. The space changes with the sections (the hall 3.5 s in the bed, 2.2 s in the drops, 5 s in the break, its shimmer rising through the break), and the call's taken-apart bars go into a cloud of grains that feeds the space. The first drop is a rolling bass on F answered by the acid, then by the squeaks; the second a gallop over F and B flat answered by the sync lead, which then doubles the call an octave up. On listening to the first mix: directionally good, much better than Sporadic, but its overall sound quality and its mix were still poor: the sounds lacked definition, clear separation and space.

So it is remixed with the DAW's new tools (F35, F24, F22), and the words on listening above are about the mix before this one. On listening to the remix: better, and no longer goa, with the right atmosphere, but the bass and the kick lack Scorb's round warmth and punch, synth licks, rhythmic sequences and sampling are lacking, the mix is improved but weak, and the composition is weak. For definition, the kick, bass, hats, call and acid have transient shapers, the kick and acid clip, the bass goes through tape, the call folds in parallel, and the hats have more metal and less noise. For separation, EQ carves the bass's low mids, the pads under the call's presence and every return's highs. For space, the returns are darker than what feeds them and wide only above their low mids, the pads wide above 300 Hz, the master mono under 110 Hz. The bed, groove and builds are turned down under the drops. The numbers: the first drop's sections are the loudest (−9.2 LUFS), the second's a little quieter (−9.8 to −10), and the groove's loudest 12 s, bars 21-28, still measure −9.0, so they are its main section: 4 of 28 measures typical and 21 more in range, nearest to Pareidolia / Sol Invictus, Thundermental Redeux and Yodyssey. Three are past all of Scorb there: the low band (−3.9 dB, theirs at least −3.4), the micro-dynamics (4.4 dB, theirs 5.4 to 12.3) and peak to loudness (10.1 dB, theirs at most 10). In the drops the low band is in range (−2.9 and −3.1), and the highs no longer past theirs (0.63 and 0.61, at most 0.702). The first drop's roll is still flat (4.5 dB), and the second drop is the quietest of its set (−9.6 LUFS, 10.7 dB peak to loudness). Tails are longer than they were but short (36 and 28 ms, median 107) and it is still dry in the drops (ambience −22.4 and −22.1 dB, median −14.6).
