Self-assessment

Written 2026-10-29, against Infected Mushroom, 2000–2001: Classical Mushroom (2000) and B.P. Empire (2001): 30 s previews of 14 tracks.

Black Spore Fugue has the frame of the records it is after: its tempo, its balance from the sub to the upper mids, how busy its rhythm is and the holes between its hits all sit inside the 14 Classical Mushroom and B.P. Empire previews, nearest to Mush Mushi and B.P. Empire. It parts from them in the master and the image. It is louder and more squashed than all 14 (7.1 dB from peak to loudness, against 10.3 to 19.4), so its drops have no punch in reserve; and from 112 Hz to 1.8 kHz it is narrower than every one of them, under a top 6 dB duller than their median. That is most likely the dryness heard in it: its tails ring as long as theirs, but below 1.8 kHz they sit in the middle, and the bright wash their hats, cymbals and reverbs make is missing. It is also a 30 s sketch on one figure, its bars changing less than 13 of the 14 references'. The numbers can't judge a sound or a melody; on listening, those are what keep it furthest from top-tier psytrance.
