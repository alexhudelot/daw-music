Self-assessment

Written 2026-10-31, against Deviant Species: 30 s previews of Deviant Species.

Haustorium is written against Sclerotium's verdict that its bass was jarring, basic and repetitive. The bass stays on F, held or in short figures, a round steady body with its movement in a separate layer above it. The harmony moves to the upper parts, in cells of a held note and its semitone neighbour, held for up to 32 bars while their timbre drifts. Riffs are events followed by a wet space, and one invented sound, the spore, runs from a breath to a throat to metal across the 280 bars. Stems put the upper parts and the bass near Deviant Species and Scorb in level, and the bass's harmonics close to theirs. Still off: micro-dynamics at bars 213 to 248 (4.6 dB, their minimum 5.0), 7 to 10 distinct upper sounds against their 4 to 6, and too much of the upper parts' brightness moving at the beat (40 to 43%, theirs 26%). On listening: a massive improvement. Good compositions, hooks, leads, variations and interesting basslines, a good mix and sound separation, and the track takes you on a journey. But the sounds are quite simple and still recall old-school goa, as do the melodies, and the sound design is clean but not modern.
